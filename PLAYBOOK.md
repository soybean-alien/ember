# Ember listing playbook

The rules every listing and every reply follows. Claude writes the listings in chat from this file; the seller pastes them into Ember.

## What to give Claude for each item

- Two or three photos.
- The manufacturer's spec page (link).
- Three or four facts only the owner knows: condition, why it's being sold, what's included, any flaw.
- The asking price, and this week's price new if known.

## Truth first

- Use only facts from the seller, the spec page or the photos.
- No invented deadlines, buyers, prices or condition claims.
- Name every flaw. A flaw the buyer finds at pickup ends the sale; a flaw named in the listing builds trust.

## The three levers, each used once and quietly

- **Value is arithmetic.** This week's new price with tax, the saving in dollars, and an invitation to check it.
- **Emotion is specifics.** One scene of the item in use, and owner details nobody could fake.
- **Urgency is logistics.** "I only have the one, and I don't hold items." Nothing more.

## Voice

- Plain, short, first person. The tone of a careful owner.
- No exclamation marks, capitals for emphasis, emoji or long dashes.
- Never "OBO", "must go", "need gone", a bare "firm", or any mention of the economy.
- Precise prices. "I priced it fairly so nobody has to haggle."

## Fixed order

1. Title: exact brand, model and variant, then condition. No price. eBay titles stay within 80 characters.
2. First two lines: condition and the price arithmetic.
3. One scene.
4. Why it's being sold, what's included, the flaw.
5. Spec block: exact figures, model number, "full model, not a holiday-special version", and the specs a buyer should match.
6. The gap against the newer model, named once.

**Items that are not electronics** (tools, cameras, backpacks, clothes and the rest) keep the same order. Only steps 5 and 6 change:
- The spec block becomes the facts a buyer of that kind of item checks: brand, exact model or style name, size and measurements, material, colour, what's included (batteries, charger, case, lenses, straps), and for cameras the shutter count if known.
- Step 6 becomes the price of the same item new today. Leave it out if the item is no longer sold new.
- Name the search words a buyer would type in the title: brand, model, size.
7. Close: one unit, no holds, the area, the times, payment, look it over before paying, and "message me a time that works" as the last line.

## Two registers

- The body is plain enough for a newcomer.
- The spec block is exact enough for an enthusiast.
- The three specs that matter most are translated, not diluted.

## Checks before posting

- It still reads well on the fifth re-read, when the buyer is hunting for a reason not to buy.
- Newcomer check: is there any word they wouldn't know?
- Enthusiast check: is any question left open?

## Who it leads with

- Pick one: decided buyer, enthusiast, gift buyer or newcomer, from the item, the platform and the date.
- Write a second version for the runner-up. Swap it in after three quiet days (Ember flags this).

## Date angles

- Before Black Friday (11/27/2026), Black Friday weekend, and December each get their own angle.
- Never make claims about retailers.

## Replies

| When they | Do this |
|---|---|
| Contact you | Reply within minutes and offer two pickup times, the earliest today. |
| Ask a question | Answer it exactly, then offer a time. |
| Counteroffer | One calm reply: the price is fair, it's the asking price, and the offer of a time stands. |
| Say "let me think" | Leave them be. One friendly check-in a day later, then nothing. |
| Go quiet | One nudge a day later, then let it go. |
| Accept | Press **Accepted** in Ember and paste the wrap-up message. |
| Have a time set | Confirm with a detail that shows it's being prepared. Message an hour before. |
| Arrive | Hand the item over first. They look it over, then pay: cash or Apple Pay. |
| Have had it two days | Paste the thank-you message. It asks for feedback once, with no pressure. |

## Limits

- No wording wins every buyer.
- Wording cannot rescue a price that is less than about 20 to 30 percent under new.
